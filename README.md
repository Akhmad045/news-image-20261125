A broadsheet front page summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026 in one
image. Lead: a Houthi drone hits Riyadh's tallest tower, the PIF Tower,
injuring 11 — the second strike in five days. Also: Ukraine sinks the
Russian corvette Merkury off Libya; the CIA and ODNI are breached; Huawei
is found guilty on 10 counts in New York; the Solomon Islands sign a
security pact with Australia; Senegal get their AFCON title back; and
Australian inflation jumps to 4.9%.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. The image is drawn at build time by `draw.py`
(Pillow) and is byte-identical on every run.
